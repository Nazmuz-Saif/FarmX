# 🌱 FarmX
### Navigating the Future of Urban Farming — "A Smart Aquaponics Approach"

> A smart urban farming startup concept that transforms unused rooftop spaces into sustainable aquaponics farms using IoT-based monitoring — producing fresh, chemical-free vegetables and fish for urban households in Dhaka.

> 📌 *Developed as a Business & Entrepreneurship project at University of Asia Pacific (UAP), Semester 3.1*

## 💡 The Problem

Urban areas like Dhaka face:
- 🚨 Food safety concerns — pesticides & chemicals in produce
- 🏙️ Thousands of unused rooftop spaces in the city
- 🚚 Long supply chains reducing freshness & traceability
- 📈 Rising demand for organic, chemical-free food

---

## 🌿 The Solution

FarmX converts unused rooftops into **smart aquaponics farms** that simultaneously produce fish and vegetables in a single water-efficient ecosystem — monitored by IoT sensors in real time.

**Tagline:** *"সবুজ যখন ঘরের অতিথি।"*
**Promise:** *Rooftop to Table in 45 minutes*

---

## ✨ Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🐟 Smart Aquaponics | Fish + vegetable cultivation in one ecosystem |
| 📡 IoT Monitoring | Real-time pH, temperature, oxygen & water level tracking |
| 🚫 Chemical-Free | No harmful fertilizers or pesticides |
| 🏠 Rooftop Utilization | Converts idle urban rooftops into productive farms |
| 🚀 Dual Revenue Model | Food sales + rooftop setup services |
| ⚡ Fast Delivery | Direct rooftop-to-door delivery within 45 minutes |
